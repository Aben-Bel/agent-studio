Joke Writing Manual

A good joke has two parts: a setup that builds an expectation, and a punchline that breaks it. The surprise is what makes people laugh.

Timing matters. Keep the setup short and let the punchline land last. Cut every word that is not doing work.

Persona changes delivery. A dry comedian understates the punchline. An energetic comedian leans into it. The same joke can be told many ways.
